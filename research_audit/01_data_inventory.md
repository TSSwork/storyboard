**Reading guide.** "Independent?" says whether the source adds information beyond another source. Three apparent sources turned out to be duplicates of each other (see §Independence).

## Independence
* **FAOSTAT "Rice" production = DCS paddy**: r = 1.000, ratio 1.000, 2010–2024. Not independent.
* **FAOSTAT rice import qty ≈ Comtrade 1006 netWgt**: r = 0.998 (both customs-based).
* **CBSL rice imports = Comtrade (2021–2024 identical to 0.1 kt)**; CBSL 2025 = 184.7 kt vs Comtrade 84.1 kt → Comtrade 2025 is incomplete.
* **HARTI vs CBSL** (Dambulla retail carrot, monthly): r(levels) 0.956, r(Δlog) 0.866 — same markets, partly the same underlying survey; treat as one evidence stream with two quality checks, not two independent price sources.
* World Bank rice prices are a modelled index built on market surveys; not independent of HARTI/WFP.
